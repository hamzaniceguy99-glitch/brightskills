The programs, in detail

Four programs of 6 to 10 weeks, all at your own pace, with written steps after every session.

Program 01 · The device you already own

Phone & tablet basics

We start with the device in your hand, not a general lesson. Making text and icons larger usually changes everything on its own. Then we cover finding apps, closing them, charging, and what to do when something looks different from yesterday.

  • 8 weeks
  • Complete beginners
  • 45 min a week
  • Your own device

Camera, clock, notes and the weather.

Weeks 7–8 · When it goes wrong

What to do when something changes or disappears.

Weeks 1–2 · Answering

Picking up a video call without panic.

Weeks 3–4 · Calling

Saving contacts and starting a call yourself.

Weeks 5–6 · Photos

Taking, finding and sending pictures to the family.

Program 02 · See the grandchildren

Video calls & photos

The most requested program, by a distance. We practice answering a call until it's automatic, then making one. Then photos: taking, finding and sending them. We practice together, repeatedly, until you don't need notes.

  • 6 weeks
  • Beginners
  • 45 min a week
  • A phone or tablet

Program 03 · Spot it before you click

Scams & online safety

Scammers target older adults deliberately and they are good at it. We go through the real patterns — the urgent call from “your bank”, the grandchild in trouble, the gift card request — and agree simple rules: hang up and call back on a number you look up yourself. We also set up passwords you can actually manage.

  • 8 weeks
  • All levels
  • 45 min a week
  • Nothing to buy

Start this program

Weeks 1–2 · The patterns

What real scams look and sound like, with examples.

Weeks 3–4 · The rules

Hang up, look up the number, call back. Every time.

Weeks 5–6 · Passwords

A system you can manage, written down safely at home.

Weeks 7–8 · If it happens

Who to call, what to freeze, and why it isn't your fault.

Weeks 1–3 · Email

Reading, replying, and what to ignore.

Weeks 4–6 · Websites

Searching, going back, and recognizing advertisements.

Weeks 7–8 · Appointments

Booking, confirming and adding it to a calendar.

Weeks 9–10 · Forms

Filling in, checking and submitting without losing your work.

Program 04 · Appointments and forms

Everyday tasks

The practical things that are increasingly hard to avoid: an email account you can use, booking a doctor's appointment online, filling in a form without losing it halfway, and finding your way back when a website swallows you. We write down every step so you can do it again alone.
